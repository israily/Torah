Moving your study data

Study data is stored on your device and does not sync automatically. To transfer it, use Export Full Backup (JSON) on the original device, then import that backup on the other device.

Text recognition

On iPhone, scan a passage from a photo in a daf’s notes editor. Recognition runs on your device. Please review the recognized text before saving it.
